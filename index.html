Jazz

Improvisational music emphasizing swing, syncopation, and musical conversation. Drums provide dynamic support with brushes, ride cymbal patterns, and interactive comping.

Jazz

Vibe

Sophisticated, improvisational, swinging

Tempo

60-300 BPM (highly variable)

EQ Approach

  • Kick: Natural (minimal EQ), slight 60 Hz warmth, no click boost
  • Snare: Wide-open (minimal cuts), gentle 200 Hz, 5-8 kHz for brush clarity
  • Toms: Natural resonance, no aggressive cutting

Tuning

  • Kick: Low-medium (warm, resonant)
  • Snare: Low-medium (sensitive, articulate)
  • Toms: Low tension (singing sustain)

Drumsticks

  • 7A or Jazz sticks + Brushes (Regal Tip, Vic Firth Jazz Rake)

Cymbals

  • Thin to Paper-Thin – Zildjian K Constantinople, Istanbul Mehmet, Sabian HH (dark, complex)

Reggae

Jamaican music characterized by offbeat rhythm guitar (the "skank"), emphasis on beats 2 & 4, and deep, steady bass. Drums play a laid-back one-drop pattern.

Reggae

Vibe:

Relaxed, groove-heavy, bass-driven

Tempo:

60-90 BPM

EQ Approach

  • Kick: Deep 50-60 Hz (steady pulse), cut 200-400 Hz, minimal click
  • Snare: Boost 150-250 Hz (body), gentle 3-5 kHz (rim cross-stick tone)
  • Toms: Natural, minimal processing

Tuning

  • Kick: Low (deep, controlled)
  • Snare: Medium-low (fat, dry)
  • Toms: Low-medium (warm)

Drumsticks

  • 5A or Hot Rods – lighter touch for groove dynamics

Cymbals

  • Medium – Zildjian A, Sabian AA (controlled wash)

Rock

High-energy music built on electric guitars, bass, and drums. Emphasizes strong backbeat (snare on 2 & 4), powerful dynamics, and driving rhythm. Originated 1950s-60s.

Rock

Vibe:

Energetic, rebellious, guitar-driven

Tempo:

90-140 BPM

EQ Approach

  • Kick: Boost 60-80 Hz (thump), cut 300-500 Hz (mud), boost 3-5 kHz (beater click)
  • Snare: Boost 200 Hz (body), boost 4 kHz (crack), high-pass at 80 Hz
  • Toms: Slight boost at fundamentals, cut 400 Hz, boost 3 kHz for attack

Tuning

  • Kick: Medium-low (tight sustain, punchy)
  • Snare: Medium-high (crisp, open ring)
  • Toms: Descending intervals (4ths/5ths between toms)

Drumsticks

  • 5A or 5B (Vic Firth, Promark) – balanced weight, good rimshots

Cymbals

  • Medium to Heavy weight – Zildjian A Series, Sabian AA, Paiste 2002

Trap

Hip-hop subgenre with booming 808 bass, rapid hi-hat rolls, and syncopated rhythms. Drums are often programmed/sampled with heavy sub-bass and crisp snares.

Trap

Vibe:

Dark, bass-heavy, modern

Tempo:

130-170 BPM (double-time feel)

EQ Approach

  • Kick: Massive 40-60 Hz (sub-bass), scoop 200-500 Hz completely, boost 2-4 kHz (punch)
  • Snare: Cut below 150 Hz, huge boost 200-250 Hz (body), boost 5-8 kHz (snap), add 10+ kHz (air)
  • Toms: Often minimal or replaced with samples

Tuning

  • Kick: Low (long sustain for sub-bass blend)
  • Snare: High (tight, crisp)
  • Toms: Medium (often tuned to key of track)

Drumsticks

  • 5A – though often triggers/samples are used

Cymbals

  • Hi-hats dominate – Crisp, tight (Zildjian A Custom, Sabian AAX)

Worship

Contemporary Christian music designed for congregational singing. Features dynamic builds, emotional swells, and space for vocals. Drums support rather than dominate.

Worship

Vibe:

Uplifting, atmospheric, supportive

Tempo:

70-130 BPM

EQ Approach

  • Kick: Gentle 50-60 Hz boost, cut 250 Hz, subtle 2 kHz (less aggressive than rock)
  • Snare: Boost 150-200 Hz (warmth), 3 kHz (gentle crack), less top-end than rock
  • Toms: Natural tone, minimal EQ, smooth sustain

Tuning

  • Kick: Medium (warm, controlled decay)
  • Snare: Medium (musical tone, not overly crispy)
  • Toms: Medium tension, resonant

Drumsticks

  • 5A – lighter touch for dynamics

Cymbals

  • Medium-Thin to Medium weight – Zildjian K series, Istanbul Agop, Sabian HHX (warm, washy)

Latin

Rhythm-focused music rooted in Afro-Caribbean traditions. Features syncopated patterns (clave), complex polyrhythms, and heavy percussion.

Latin

Vibe:

Rhythmic, danceable, percussive

Tempo:

80-200+ BPM (varies by style)

EQ Approach

  • Kick: Punchy 60-80 Hz, cut 300 Hz, boost 2-3 kHz (articulation)
  • Snare: Bright (boost 200-300 Hz, 5-7 kHz for rim clarity)
  • Toms: Open and resonant (minimal damping)

Tuning

  • Kick: Medium-high (fast response)
  • Snare: High (tight, responsive for ghost notes)
  • Toms: Medium-high (melodic intervals)

Drumsticks

  • Timbale sticks or 5A – lighter for speed

Cymbals

  • Thin to Medium – Zildjian K, Meinl Byzance (complex wash)

Pop

Popular mainstream music with catchy melodies, repetitive hooks, and polished production. Drums are tight, quantized, and radio-ready.

Pop

Vibe:

Catchy, accessible, commercial

Tempo:

100-130 BPM

EQ Approach

  • Kick: Tight low-end (60-70 Hz), aggressive cut at 400 Hz, boost 4-6 kHz (modern click)
  • Snare: Boost 200 Hz, heavy boost 4-5 kHz (crisp), add 10 kHz (air)
  • Toms: Tight/minimal sustain, focus on attack (3-5 kHz)

Tuning

  • Kick: Medium-high (punchy, fast decay)
  • Snare: High (crisp, controlled)
  • Toms: Medium-high (short sustain)

Drumsticks

  • 5A – precision and speed

Cymbals

  • Thin to Medium – Zildjian A Custom, Sabian AAX (bright, cutting)

Metal

Aggressive, intense music featuring distorted guitars, complex rhythms, and powerful drums. Often uses double bass pedals and blast beats.

Metal

Vibe:

Aggressive, heavy, technical

Tempo:

120-200+ BPM.

EQ Approach

  • Kick: Deep boost 50-60 Hz (sub-thump), scoop 300-600 Hz heavily, boost 3-5 kHz (double-bass clarity)
  • Snare: Cut 200-400 Hz (tighten), boost 1 kHz (body), huge boost 5-8 kHz (crack)
  • Toms: Tight fundamentals, aggressive attack boost 4-6 kHz

Tuning

  • Kick: Low-medium (controlled, tight for double bass)
  • Snare: High (extreme crack, minimal ring)
  • Toms: Low-medium (powerful but controlled)

Drumsticks

  • 5B or 2B (Vic Firth Metal series) – durability and power

Cymbals

  • Heavy to Extra Heavy – Zildjian A Heavy, Sabian AAX Metal, Meinl Classics Custom Extreme
